Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone, and in your own device backups if you use them. Kept has no accounts and no servers.

How do I restore Kept Pro on a new iPhone?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases and sign in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

What are the free limits?

Free includes up to 10 items and 2 photos per item, plus PDF export without photos. Kept Pro is a one-time purchase that removes the limits, and adds PDF export with photos and CSV export.

How do I delete everything?

Go to Settings → Delete All Data. This cannot be undone.
