Privacy Policy

Kept for iPhone · Effective November 10, 2026

Kept is a private home inventory app. This policy explains what happens to your information. The short version: everything you add to Kept stays on your iPhone, and we collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Kept has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. The developer never receives your items, photos, receipts, documents, values or any other data.

Where your data lives

Items, photos, receipts and documents are stored only in Kept's private storage on your device. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, Apple includes Kept's data in that backup under your own Apple Account, as it does for any app. We cannot see or access those backups.

Camera and photos

Kept asks for camera access only when you choose to take a photo of an item, receipt or document. Choosing photos from your library uses Apple's photo picker, which gives Kept access only to the pictures you select. Photos are copied into Kept's storage and are never uploaded.

Exporting and sharing

When you create a PDF or CSV and share it, the file goes wherever you send it (for example Mail, Messages or Files). That is your choice, and the file is then governed by that service's own policy.

Purchases

Kept Pro is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details and no personal information about you. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Deleting your data

You can delete a single item at any time, or erase everything with Settings → Delete All Data. Deleting the app also removes its stored data from your device.

Children

Kept does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated date.
